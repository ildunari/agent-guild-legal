Agent Guild is a native iOS client for an official Hermes Agent dashboard that you choose and control. The app does not provide a hosted Hermes service or create an account with the app developer.

How to reach Kosta

Privacy questions, support reports, and App Store review questions can be filed as an issue on this public repository:

https://github.com/ildunari/agent-guild-legal/issues

Include the app version and a short description of what happened. Do not include tokens, passwords, session cookies, private server URLs, or other secrets.

What the app is

Guild on your iPhone talks to a Hermes dashboard you operate, or that someone else operates for you. Chat, files, and credentials stay between your device and that server. The developer does not host your conversations.

  • No in-app account with the developer
  • No advertising or third-party analytics
  • Privacy policy: ildunari.github.io/agent-guild-legal/privacy

Guild is the in-app name. Agent Guild is the App Store and home-screen name.
